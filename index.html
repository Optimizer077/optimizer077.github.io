From circuits and embedded systems to vision intelligence and data.

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Artificial Intelligence at Kyung Hee University Global Campus, in the Machine Learning and Visual Computing (MLVC) Lab under Professor Sung Ho Bae. I currently work on vision-language navigation for drones, diffusion models for images and language, and MRI reconstruction. My earlier work covers implicit neural representations and image restoration.

I have published at AAAI and in Infrared Physics & Technology, with collaborations at Adobe Research and Télécom Paris. Previously I was a Research Assistant at CISS-Global, ITU Lahore, under Dr. Qasim Mehmood, and hold a B.Sc. in Electronics Engineering from UET Taxila. Outside research I enjoy reading, traveling and building things.

Learning these days

What I am studying right now, beyond my own papers.

course · Stanford CS 329Ain progress

Self-Improving AI Agents

Working through Stanford's CS 329A, taught by Azalia Mirhoseini and Aakanksha Chowdhery, with James Le's write-up of its nine lectures as a guide. The thread running through it: agents improve by generating many candidate answers, verifying them, and learning from the ones that pass.

The nine lectures
  1. What makes an agent self-improving; inference-time scaling
  2. Test-time compute, repeated sampling and search
  3. Verifiers: outcome and process reward models
  4. Learning from tool and environment feedback; self-critique
  5. Planning, tree search and parallel execution
  6. Train-time scaling, reinforcement learning and GRPO
  7. Large-scale search and deep-research agents
  8. Evaluating agents on long-horizon tasks
  9. Open problems: verifiers for hard-to-verify domains, self-generated curricula, intelligence per watt

Projects

Research and industry projects I have worked on.

Vision-Language Navigation for Drones Ongoing Research

Developing a vision-language navigation system for drones (UAVs): the aerial agent follows natural-language instructions, with lightweight VLMs for spatial and semantic reasoning.

  • MRI Acceleration and Reconstruction Ongoing Research

    In collaboration with AiLive and NYU — diffusion models and multi-modal frameworks for faster, accurate clinical MRI diagnostics.

  • Video Quality Assessment for Streaming Media

    With KEI Media — perceptual video quality benchmarking and no-reference quality assessment metrics for streaming platforms.

  • Microscopic Malaria Detection Research

    Low-cost AI diagnostic system with Lahore General Hospital (CISS-Global) — virtual staining, image enhancement, and low-resource detection.

  • AI-based Sports Card Grading Acquired by Beckett Media

    Algorithm lead at Due Dilly — automated PSA/Beckett-standard grading via large-scale data curation and visual centering networks. Startup acquired by Beckett Media.

    PID-controlled line-following robot on ATMEGA328P-PU. 1st place nationally at AirTech 2017.

    NIE Islamabad internship — inductive loop sensors with adaptive signal control based on vehicle speed and time-of-day.
